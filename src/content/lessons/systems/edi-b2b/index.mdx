<Step id="problem" name="EDI from a retailer" tag="PROBLEM">
A large retailer sends you purchase orders as **X12** documents over **AS2**: encrypted, signed, and expecting acknowledgments. Your ERP wants its own XML.

Logic Apps supports AS2, X12 and EDIFACT. The Microsoft Learn docs document each piece; this lesson puts them in one flow of our design.
</Step>

<Step id="account" name="The integration account">
An **integration account** is a container for B2B artifacts: **trading partners**, **agreements**, **certificates**, **schemas** and **maps**.

In Standard you can keep schemas and maps on the logic app itself, but you **still need an integration account** to store partners and agreements, which the AS2, X12 and EDIFACT operations use. Whether you link it or create a connection depends on the connector. It must be in the same subscription and region. Avoid the **Free** tier in production: it isn't covered by the SLA.
</Step>

<Step id="partners" name="Partners and agreements">
A **partner** is a business identity: a **qualifier**, such as D-U-N-S or a mutually defined one, and a **value**. An **agreement** connects a **host** partner, always your organization, with a **guest** partner, the retailer. It sets how inbound and outbound messages are handled, from the host's point of view.
</Step>

<Step id="as2" name="Receive: AS2 Decode">
The built-in **AS2 (v2)** connector has actions but **no triggers**, so the flow starts with another trigger that receives the message, such as Request (our choice). **AS2 Decode** then decrypts, verifies the signature, decompresses, rejects duplicate message IDs, and generates a synchronous or asynchronous **MDN**, the AS2 acknowledgment.
</Step>

<Step id="x12" name="Validate: Decode X12">
The built-in **Decode X12** action converts the flat file to XML and validates it against the agreement: the envelope, the schemas, EDI rules, and **duplicate control numbers**. With the option to split the interchange into transaction sets, valid ones go to `goodMessages` and failed ones to `badMessages`.

If configured, it also generates acknowledgments: a **technical** acknowledgment (TA1) and a **functional** acknowledgment (997).
</Step>

<Step id="transform" name="Transform for the ERP">
Each good transaction set is now XML. Transform it to the ERP's format with an **XSLT** map or **Data Mapper**, validate the result, and send it on, ideally through a queue as in the order pipeline.
</Step>

<Step id="outbound" name="Send: encode">
Outbound documents go the other way: **Encode X12** turns your XML into a flat file, resolving the agreement from sender and receiver, and **AS2 Encode** signs, encrypts and compresses, with MDN support for acknowledgments. For EDIFACT partners, the EDIFACT encode and decode actions are similar, with **CONTRL** acknowledgments, though the built-in EDIFACT Decode has documented gaps, such as no duplicate control-number check.
</Step>

<Step id="tracking" name="Tracking" tag="TIP">
For Standard, B2B tracking is in **preview**: it needs a **Premium** integration account and an **Azure Data Explorer** database, and currently tracks only **X12 and AS2** built-in actions. Its dashboard helps follow acknowledgments such as MDNs and 997s.
</Step>

<Step id="recap" name="Recap" tag="RECAP">
- The **integration account** holds partners, agreements and certificates; Standard still needs it for EDI.
- **Agreements** link your host partner to a guest partner.
- **AS2 Decode** checks security and sends the **MDN**; **Decode X12** validates and splits.
- If configured, acknowledge with **TA1/997** (X12) or **CONTRL** (EDIFACT).
- Transform with XSLT or Data Mapper; encode on the way out.
</Step>
