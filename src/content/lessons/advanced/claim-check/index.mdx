<Step id="problem" name="Too big for the queue" tag="PROBLEM">
Suppliers send invoices as **40 MB PDFs**. Two workflows need them, and they talk through a Service Bus queue.

But a Service Bus message on the Standard tier can be at most **256 KB**. The invoice won't fit.
</Step>

<Step id="pattern" name="The idea">
**Don't send the file. Send where the file is.**

Like a coat check at a theatre: you hand in the coat and get a small ticket. The ticket travels; the coat stays safe. Microsoft calls this the **Claim Check** pattern.
</Step>

<Step id="limits" name="Why the queue says no">
- Service Bus Standard: **256 KB** per message. Premium: **1 MB** by default, raise it up to **100 MB** per queue or topic (over AMQP).
- The **managed** Service Bus connector: **1 MB**, even on Premium. Large messages need the **built-in** connector.
- Big messages also slow a queue down, even when they fit.
</Step>

<Step id="sequence" name="How it flows">
1. The sender **saves the invoice** in Blob Storage.
2. **Only when that succeeds**, it sends a small message with the blob's path.
3. The receiver reads the message, then **fetches the invoice** from Blob Storage.
</Step>

<Step id="build" name="Build it in Logic Apps">
Our build in Standard, with built-in actions:

**Sender workflow**
1. **Upload blob to storage container**: the invoice.
2. **Send message** (Service Bus): body `{ "blob": "invoices/INV-778.pdf" }`.

**Receiver workflow**
1. Trigger: **When messages are available in a queue**.
2. **Read blob content**, using the path from the message.
3. Process the invoice.
</Step>

<Step id="eventgrid" name="Skip the message (option)">
You can let the upload itself be the ticket. Microsoft's samples use **Event Grid**: saving the blob raises a **BlobCreated** event, and the blob's URL in that event is the claim check.

In Standard, the built-in trigger **When a blob is added or updated** is a simpler way to start the receiver from the upload (ours).
</Step>

<Step id="lifecycle" name="Who deletes the file?" tag="PROBLEM">
The message leaves the queue once it's completed. The file doesn't.

Decide **which workflow deletes it**, for example the receiver with **Delete a blob** when it's done (ours). And never delete a file that a waiting message still points to.
</Step>

<Step id="security" name="Keep it secure" tag="TIP">
Don't put keys or tokens in the message: just the path. Let each workflow reach Blob Storage with its own **managed identity** (ours).
</Step>

<Step id="when" name="When to use it">
**Use it** when files are bigger than your queue allows, or too sensitive to sit in a queue.

**Skip it** when a higher tier fits the message, such as Service Bus Premium, or when every millisecond counts: fetching the file adds a step.
</Step>

<Step id="recap" name="Recap" tag="RECAP">
- Send **where the file is**, not the file.
- Save first, **then** send the message.
- Build: Upload blob → Send message; Read blob content on the other side.
- Decide who **deletes** the file. No keys in the message.
</Step>
